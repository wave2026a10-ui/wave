WAVE protocol docs

Every idea has a wave.

WAVE is a non-custodial, multi-chain launchpad for BSC, Solana, and Robinhood Chain.

Overview

Creators configure a token, review immutable launch parameters, and ask their wallet to deploy. Traders interact directly with contracts. WAVE never holds user funds or private keys.

Core principle: every important number is visible before signing: supply, fees, liquidity, curve, permissions, and protocol revenue.

Launch lifecycle

01 · ConfigureSet name, ticker, image, supply, chain, curve, liquidity, and fixed Creator Tax.
02 · ReviewCheck mint authority, pause controls, fee caps, liquidity, and compatibility.
03 · LaunchThe creator signs from a compatible wallet. The launch is recorded on-chain.

After launch, supply and fee terms are immutable. Creator Tax cannot be raised later.

Fees & WAVE buyback

Each trade has a fixed 1% standard fee. Creator Tax is optional, capped at 2%, shown before trading, and fixed at launch.

50% · CreatorCreator fee recipient.
30% · WAVE protocolBuyback, security, infrastructure, and operations.
20% · ReserveLiquidity and protocol risk reserve.

80% of WAVE protocol revenue is reserved for WAVE buybacks, 10% for security/infrastructure, and 10% for operations. Before WAVE launches, revenue remains in a transparent Treasury. After launch, buybacks execute through bounded TWAP transactions.

No price promise: buybacks and burns do not guarantee that WAVE or any launch token will increase in value.

Networks

BSC and Robinhood Chain use EVM-compatible contracts. Solana uses SPL Token programs. WAVE keeps the experience unified while preserving each chain's native wallet and transaction model.

Robinhood Chain mainnet: Chain ID 4663 · Native gas: ETH · RPC: official RPC

Safety & risk disclosures

Token names and images can be copied. Always verify the contract address and network. Tokens may be volatile, illiquid, or lose all value. Smart contracts, wallets, bridges, RPCs, and indexers can fail. WAVE is non-custodial and does not provide investment advice.
